Which side carries more weight?  
The bear’s case leans on present, verifiable numbers (declining regional sales, legal liabilities, valuation multiples) while the bull’s case hinges largely on future optionality (robotaxi, FSD at human-outperform levels) that is still unproven and un-monetised. Until those optionalities become revenues rather than narratives, the risk/reward profile is skewed to the downside.

My call: SELL / UNDERWEIGHT
